# November 2026 development ledger: early records, part 1

See [Ostrog release and development history](../../RELEASE-HISTORY.md) for the timeline and credits.

This installment covers 111 source records closed or merged between November 1 and midday November 8, 2026 (UTC). It is a verified subset of that window. Related records are grouped by subject. A merged development change is not evidence of a published release or of inclusion in the current public build. An issue closure does not establish why it closed.

### N01-01 · Floods and food shortages could leave a traceable chain

A proposal connected spoiled food, pests and sickness back to flooding. Related work explored how an exposed roof, a lost healer or a lean winter could make a local problem spread. The aim was a chain the Chronicle could explain, not a string of unrelated bad rolls.

The corresponding design note asked for small failures to compound through causally linked systems, rather than merely making events more frequent.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-02 · Hidden traits and discoveries

Two proposals explored unusual dreams and a latent sense for stone. Both treated discovery as something that emerges under particular conditions rather than a power the viewer chooses from a menu.

*Records: 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-03 · The first hours should not feel the same for everyone

An arrival change tied the shock of entering the valley to disposition and survival pressure. A related test proposal fixed the scenery and starting conditions so it could compare people rather than compare two different worlds.

The arrival design argued against a neutral start: panic, urgent survival action and quieter reactions should differ by person.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-04 · The same world, under the same build

One timing-test proposal allowed more room for a slow historical pass; another distinguished CPU time from contention. A reported split between continuous and resumed play turned out, in the follow-up investigation, to compare different builds. A longer matched save/resume guard and a separate thread-count reproducibility recheck were recorded. A later audit distinguished source progress from release evidence.

*Records: 2 closed issues; 5 merged development changes.*

### N01-05 · Warnings and know-how travel between people

One change was proposed to carry selective, imperfect warnings into the main development line. Another limited who passes on practical knowledge and who trusts the teller. Companion notes recorded these assumptions alongside the work on judgment and perception.

The merged warning slice kept the teller and triggering event attached to the warning; trust changed urgency, and resentment could suppress or distort what was shared. The broader teaching proposal was a design direction, not evidence that all social learning was implemented.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 4 merged development changes.*

### N01-06 · People stop choosing impossible plans

A long run surfaced costly repeats such as multiple people heading for the same food. The proposed action-level checks stopped selecting known failures before anyone set out. The investigation established some causes, not an explanation for every future failed plan.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 1 merged development change.*

### N01-07 · Useful things beneath the quarry

Proposals gave communities concrete reasons to dig deeper and tied disturbances below to activity near the workings. The intended warning before trouble was part of making a dangerous excavation legible.

*Records: 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-08 · Walls should close a usable ring

A fortification fix addressed rings enclosing inaccessible ground and allowed a house to keep its own space instead of running a wall through it.

Long runs found that some wall projects stayed open and that a relocated camp could keep an obsolete wall. The merged relocation fix closed abandoned wall projects when the whole camp moved, while a split that still used the old site kept its project. That fix did not explain every earlier failure to close a ring.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-09 · Choosing a valley should have visible consequences

The selection flow was challenged for reusing a seed, confusing one place with another and losing its marker against pale terrain. Proposals added varied valleys, previews of the selected place and clearer markers. The opening flow also proposed showing world history before entry, while the map during play showed people who were visiting other settlements.

*Records: 5 closed issues; 4 merged development changes.*

### N01-10 · Explain decisions without turning them into orders

Decision recording and the in-game Why? view were proposed to expose what someone weighed and what they put off. A follow-up improved the diagnostic stream so repeated and abandoned plans could be examined without drowning in unrelated messages.

*Records: 2 closed issues; 3 merged development changes.*

### N01-11 · Absence is not the same as a body at home

A reported caravan loss incorrectly left a local body and rite. The proposed fix distinguished mourning someone away from attending a local death. Related story work explored differing spoken accounts and graves that later people could read.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-12 · Experience changes choices and perceptions

Proposals explored how people reassess an outcome, how experience changes what they notice and how a competing need can cause an intention to slip or be forgotten. These are distinct decisions rather than one all-purpose mood change.

The merged work gave individuals bounded records of problems, candidate reactions and actual outcomes, including failed attempts. It added limited worksite clues and an experience pane without reading other people's thoughts. A separate merged slice gave new people durable dispositions whose current expression can vary with rest, stress, mood or injury. The closed design briefs describe a wider goal; these changes delivered initial pieces, not the entire model.

*Records: 6 closed issues; 7 merged development changes.*

### N01-13 · Different creatures need different kinds of bodies

A connected series proposed bodies that can be soft, made of a swarm or segmented. Injury and movement would follow that body plan rather than defaulting to skeletal anatomy; several cave creatures were used to exercise the distinctions.

The corresponding design work treated larger footprints as a separate problem from changing how a body is built. A new body type alone is not evidence that multi-tile movement, clearance and collision were delivered.

*Records: 2 closed issues; 4 merged development changes.*

### N01-14 · Record the reason for a decision

A documentation proposal separated who made a design decision, its status and the alternatives that were rejected, so later work would not silently convert a proposal into settled policy.

*Records: 1 merged development change.*

### N01-15 · Make valley replays repeatable

A review found that repeated captures could disagree because weather was drawn differently. Proposed timelapse work tied samples to fixed game times, bounded storage and made important events easier to find on the timeline.

*Records: 1 merged development change.*

### N01-16 · Beds and nighttime behavior

Early observations showed unnecessary nights outdoors and people getting up when they could rest. Related proposals made household beds discoverable and reduced needless nighttime activity; the design rationale was recorded separately.

The investigation found persistent rough-sleep stress even with beds standing. A merged fix considers beds the person can currently see and reach before choosing the ground when no known bed is available. It does not grant knowledge of hidden beds.

*Records: 2 closed issues; 4 merged development changes.*

### N01-17 · Recognize the work people actually do

One proposal derived duties from recent activity. Testing exposed a gap: collecting food and tending the sick could happen without anyone being recognized as a forager or healer. The follow-up proposed crediting those actions directly.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-18 · Everyday activity should read at a glance

Animation work explored visible breathing during sleep and clearer cues for tending and drinking. Another renderer change proposed shadows at the foot of built walls to make their height easier to read.

Merged work added eating and talking cycles tied to the actual activity and expanded checks beyond the average adult silhouette. This was a slice of making people easier to tell apart, not completion of the visual overhaul.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 4 merged development changes.*

### N01-19 · Predators still need food

Ecology work considered how grazing, hunting and scavenging interact. A follow-up addressed foxes that stayed alive after prey was gone, a concrete example of how a missing limit could leave other species persistently low.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-20 · Keep stepping the world from stalling the view

Measurements found that the client could be held up by simulation ticks. The renderer proposal separated simulation stepping from the view and had the viewer read a snapshot rather than wait for every tick in the same call.

The associated engine review recommended retaining the client engine while moving expensive work away from its scripting and scene-update path. It was not a decision to switch engines.

*Records: 2 closed issues; 2 merged development changes.*

### N01-21 · A water source has to be usable from where people stand

A run reported people dying of thirst beside available water. The follow-up identified a mismatch between the standing place offered and where the action permitted drinking, and proposed using consistent reach rules.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 1 merged development change.*

### N01-22 · Add upper rooms only when the structure supports them

Household need drove a proposal for staged upper sleeping rooms when a ground-floor annex was unavailable. Follow-ups explored using finished accessible upper rooms for work and removing upper structures when their support failed.

A merged prerequisite gave stairs real labor and material costs and made unsupported upper floors wait; it did not by itself let the settlement planner start building upstairs.

*Records: 4 merged development changes.*

### N01-23 · Prepare and recover Windows update packages

A set of proposals covered verifying update information, staging changes, recovering when installation fails and checking that packages work on Windows. These are engineering records, not proof that any particular package was distributed.

The merged watch-copy launcher refused dirty or divergent checkouts and checked the prebuilt component before installing it. Its fallback could build locally if no suitable prebuilt component existed. The closed-unmerged packaging proposal is not counted as landed; the available evidence does not establish why it was closed.

*Records: 2 closed issues; 16 merged development changes; 1 proposal closed without merging.*
