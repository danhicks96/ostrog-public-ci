# November 2026 development ledger: part 3

See [Ostrog release and development history](../../RELEASE-HISTORY.md) for the timeline and credits.

This installment covers 100 source records closed or merged November 12–14, 2026 (UTC). A development merge does not establish a public release. The record counts distinguish closed issues, merged development changes, and proposals closed without merging.

### N03-01 · Feed messages stay long enough to read

The viewer once gave a feed line a fixed lifetime in real time, which meant it represented very different stretches of valley time at different speeds. The merged change used in-game time and let severity affect how long a line stayed visible.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 1 merged development change.*

### N03-02 · Save before quitting and explain what the controls will do

Closing the window formerly discarded progress since the latest autosave. The close-window change saved before exit. Other changes made Load report when there was nothing to load, made the bar ask before replacing the current view, and showed slider values and whether autosave was off.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 3 merged development changes.*

### N03-03 · A camera suggestion is optional, not an instruction to crash

A short director response crashed the camera when it read a missing value. The merged guard handled incomplete responses; related work made the director answer during normal play and stopped treating a wild animal's death as a reason to move the camera. Bundled documentation followed the same change without creating a second feature.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 3 merged development changes.*

### N03-04 · Keep parallel tests within their thread budgets

An arrival test failed under concurrent testing. Changes in the same test lane explicitly bounded parallel worlds in the pacing and unusual-world tests. The available records do not establish that this change fixed the separate arrival test.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 1 merged development change.*

### N03-05 · Ground conditions needed both effects and honest evidence

A generated-world review showed that the earlier loose-rock change never activated in practice; documentation corrected the claim without declaring the underlying issue fixed. Other merged changes applied weather cover to fire behavior, gave rough terrain a movement effect, and made a weed compete with crops. A clarification distinguished rough terrain from rain mud, which did not slow walkers.

*Records: 1 closed issue; 5 merged development changes.*

### N03-06 · Check a real Windows install and fix what goes wrong

The development work covered a staged first-install walkthrough, the launcher's actual save location, removal of a runtime dependency, and cleanup after a finished update so the next one could commit. Tests checked Windows path handling and publish guards; the launcher was adjusted to find the actual save-write marker, and a delta slice carried changed files as patches. Documentation and delivery configuration were folded and corrected against the real install. Test and packaging evidence is not proof of a general public release.

*Records: 16 merged development changes.*

### N03-07 · Make the direction and first view clearer

A merged roadmap recorded the project's direction after a review of what already felt alive. The setup wizard also gained plainer descriptions of the valley so a viewer could understand the choice without interpreting internal vocabulary.

*Records: 2 merged development changes.*

### N03-08 · Tell viewers what happened while the valley keeps moving

Merged changes delivered the feed and event sounds during normal running, narrowed mood explanations to events actually driven by mood, and kept announcements below the heads-up display or queued behind a reading panel.

*Records: 3 merged development changes.*

### N03-09 · Let one reading panel have the viewer's attention

A centered reading panel no longer had to compete with another; Escape addressed the top one, including People and Waiting Work. The mouse and keyboard guide was moved to the left so it would not cover the settlement.

*Records: 3 merged development changes.*

### N03-10 · Follow people and events without assuming everyone is here

The People view showed who was present or away and what they were doing, with the heads-up count aligned to that scope. The World view drew on what the settlement knew, and event markers showed where a reported action happened, with limits on how long and how many remained visible.

*Records: 7 merged development changes.*

### N03-11 · Account for the files created by an install

A Windows install audit checked the files the app actually made and corrected their register. New checks kept the inventory complete; the download page took its list from the same register. Listing files did not by itself mean their contents were transmitted.

*Records: 2 merged development changes.*

### N03-12 · Measure faster drawing, then avoid rebuilding what did not change

The renderer gained static world batches and later rebuilt only changed parts on the CPU side. On-device comparisons measured frame time with the newer path both off and on. Handoff notes recorded the evidence; the benchmark does not promise performance on every setup.

*Records: 4 merged development changes.*

### N03-13 · Let discoveries spread through memory, fatigue and imperfect accounts

A first unusual ability could appear ambiguously; using rock sense took rest and exhaustion could stop it until sleep. Witnesses needed a view of the event and could teach or withhold what they learned. Later work supported fragments and disputed historical records. Tests kept dormant potential from leaking into the viewer, and the development board distinguished completed slices from a narrower remaining gem-carving task.

*Records: 11 merged development changes.*

### N03-14 · Remove repository artifacts that made review brittle

Merged housekeeping removed duplicated fix notes, refreshed generated tables, normalized a generated document's line endings and stopped tracking a machine-local link. The link guard kept that local setup detail out of tracked files.

*Records: 4 merged development changes.*

### N03-15 · Test the read models through the client

The automated viewer sweep exercised startup and then added separate guard coverage for person inspection and block inspection. Documentation tracked those increments without treating the checks as a substitute for every manual viewing scenario.

*Records: 5 merged development changes.*

### N03-16 · Make unusual settlement failures observable and recoverable

When a worker lost an assignment, it released the work it held. Other merged changes covered people leaving despite a blocked route, shelters abandoned and dug again after refill, eventual waking during a night raid, and rebuilding a wall gap over a hole. Some records added regression checks rather than new behavior; the wall-gap note corrected an earlier assumption about what would happen.

*Records: 6 merged development changes.*

### N03-17 · Let the soundtrack answer the settlement's bustle

The music presentation let a period of work gently raise the tension layer. The tracking update recorded the change; neither record changed simulation rules.

*Records: 2 merged development changes.*

### N03-18 · Make people easier to recognize at a glance

Merged work added an inspection portrait and relationship lines and fixed a display case where only one side held the relationship. A minimap proposal was closed without merging; the available record does not establish why. The portrait fix ensured its guard could fail honestly and release the test node.

*Records: 4 merged development changes; 1 proposal closed without merging.*

### N03-19 · Fold interdependent changes without counting them twice

Several development merges brought already-described fixes, tests and viewer work together to verify them against one revision. Handoff and board updates tracked the resulting evidence, restored a single current handoff and recorded who owned the next slice. Integration and documentation are tracked here separately from the underlying features.

*Records: 10 merged development changes.*

### N03-20 · Restore the Chronicle when loading an older valley

The merged load repair restored the event-history schema after loading older saves so the Chronicle did not depend on a newly created world.

*Records: 1 merged development change.*

### N03-21 · Explain why planned work is waiting

A merged read model gave the planned-work line a reason when an order could not proceed, rather than leaving the viewer to infer that nobody had noticed it.

*Records: 1 merged development change.*
