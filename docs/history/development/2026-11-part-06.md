See [Ostrog release and development history](../../RELEASE-HISTORY.md) for the timeline and credits.

This installment covers 58 additional source records closed or merged between November 9 and November 18, 2026 (UTC). It completes the November archive ledger and includes proposals that ended without merging as individual pull requests. Where the same work landed in a combined revision, it is not counted as a second delivered change. A development merge is not proof of a public release.
